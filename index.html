Who We Are

Dombivli's Trusted Plumbing & Civil Work Experts

Saish Plumbing & Civil Work Services has been serving the Dombivli-Kalyan region for over a decade with unwavering commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. We started as a small plumbing service and have grown into a full-service civil work contractor.

Our team comprises highly skilled professionals who bring expertise, precision, and dedication to every project. From minor pipe repairs to major waterproofing projects, from residential renovations to industrial installations — we handle it all with equal professionalism.

We take pride in our specialized leakage and waterproofing work with 100% guaranteed results. Our transparent pricing, timely service delivery, and use of premium materials have earned us the trust of hundreds of satisfied clients across Dombivli, Kalyan, Thane, and surrounding areas.
